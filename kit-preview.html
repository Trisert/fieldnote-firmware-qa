FULL KIT / MANIFEST

Everything needed to start a credible QA loop.

The bundle is plain files on purpose: inspect them, adapt them, commit them to your project, and delete what you do not need.

checklists/firmware-qa-checklist.mdReview surface map from requirements to release evidence.
templates/requirements-traceability.csvRequirement, verification method, evidence, owner, and status in a spreadsheet-friendly format.
templates/test-plan.mdHost, hardware-in-loop, fault-injection, and release test planning structure.
templates/bug-report.ymlGitHub Issue Form for reproducible embedded failures.
templates/issue-template.mdSmall issue template for a requirement-linked engineering task.
templates/ci-stm32.ymlCI scaffold for host tests, static analysis, firmware build, and artifact upload.
examples/README.mdAdapter notes: what to replace for your MCU, build system, test runner, and board.
Boundary: this is a starter process, not a certification package, flight-readiness decision, or safety guarantee. It must be reviewed against the actual hardware, requirements, interfaces, and applicable standards.
